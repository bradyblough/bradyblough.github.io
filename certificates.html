Certificates

Selected coursework and certifications focused on scientific computing and space science. These support my interest in mission-focused software, data processing, and building reliable tools for complex systems.

Scientific Computing with Python | freeCodeCamp

Certification covering Python fundamentals, data processing, NumPy-based numerical computation, and algorithmic problem solving (approximately 300 hours of hands-on work).

Astronomy: Exploring Time and Space | University of Arizona

Broad astronomy foundation covering observational methods, galaxies, stellar evolution, cosmology, and astrobiology.

From the Big Bang to Dark Energy | University of Tokyo

Introductory cosmology covering the evolution of the universe, dark matter, dark energy, and foundational particle physics concepts.

The Science of the Solar System | Caltech

Study of solar system formation, planetary science, and comparative planetology, including the search for life beyond Earth.

AstroTech: Science and Technology Behind Astronomical Discovery | University of Edinburgh

Overview of the tools behind modern astronomy—telescopes, detectors, and instrumentation—and how engineering choices shape outcomes.

Data-driven Astronomy | University of Sydney

Course focused on analyzing astronomical datasets using scientific Python tools, applying algorithms to extract insights, and introducing ML concepts.
